# Experiment: LLM rewrites the whole brief (rejected)

Kept as evidence for a design decision; not produced by the current pipeline.

On 2026-10-26 the pipeline asked `qwen2.5:3b` to rewrite the full template brief
into plainer English. The output passed the number guard (every number appears in
`brief_facts.json`) and kept every item, yet changed meaning in places:

| LLM wrote | Facts say | Problem |
|---|---|---|
| "satisfaction at 3.7/5 compared to **the baseline** of 3.9/5" | 3.9 is the average of *all tickets in the same 4 weeks* | Wrong comparison, invisible to a number check |
| "Customers are waiting longer to be resolved (50 hours total)" | 50 h vs 31 h for all tickets | Comparison dropped; "50 hours" alone means nothing |
| "Agents file real complaints at the same rate in every channel" | …file real complaints *as Unclassified*… | Sentence lost its meaning |

**Decision:** the brief body is the deterministic template; the model only writes
the two-to-three sentence "Bottom line", which is short enough to check and where
fluent wording matters most. See `docs/PLANNING.md` §4.8.

The rejected output, verbatim:
